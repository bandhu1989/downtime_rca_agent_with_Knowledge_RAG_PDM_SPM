# Simulated SOP — Rectifier Overtemperature
**ID:** SIM-SOP-REC-001 | **Rev:** 0.1 | **Training / PoC only**

> Rectifier work is restricted to authorized electrical personnel. Never open energized equipment or alter charging recipes/protection settings.

**Trigger:** PLC_E330, high temperature indication or repeated thermal trip.

**Operator response**
1. Record rectifier ID, alarm, indicated temperature, operating mode and time.
2. Observe from safe distance for blocked external airflow, fan noise, smoke or smell.
3. Follow approved alarm response and notify supervisor/electrical maintenance.
4. Do not open covers, touch terminals, change charging settings or repeatedly reset.
5. Escalate smoke, burning smell or visible damage immediately.

**Authorized electrical maintenance**
1. Follow OEM shutdown and site electrical LOTO/permit.
2. Verify isolation and stored-energy controls by approved method.
3. Inspect airflow path, filters and fans per OEM.
4. Authorized specialist checks connections, cooling components, internal condition and event logs.
5. Validate sensing and operating duty against approved criteria.
6. Repair with approved parts; do not modify protective settings.
7. Complete approved functional test and controlled return to service.
8. Monitor temperature trend and record evidence.

**Close-out:** document cause, cooling/sensing verification, protective-function status and authorized acceptance.
