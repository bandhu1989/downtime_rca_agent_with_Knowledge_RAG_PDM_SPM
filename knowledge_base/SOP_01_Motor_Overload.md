# Simulated SOP — Motor Overload
**ID:** SIM-SOP-MTR-001 | **Rev:** 0.1 | **Training / PoC only**

> Not an approved site SOP. Follow current OEM/site instructions, LOTO and permit controls. Never repeatedly reset or bypass protection.

**Trigger:** PLC_A102, elevated motor-current trend or operator report.

**Operator response**
1. Follow the machine's approved alarm/stop response.
2. Record asset, alarm, timestamp, product/order and machine state.
3. Observe from safe position for unusual noise, smell, obstruction or vibration.
4. Check HMI and upstream/downstream status without entering guarded areas.
5. Notify supervisor and authorized maintenance; do not repeatedly reset.

**Authorized maintenance**
1. Confirm asset and current OEM procedure.
2. Apply approved LOTO and verify safe energy state before intrusive work.
3. Inspect mechanical load path for binding, obstruction, damage or lubrication issue.
4. Authorized electrical specialist reviews motor, drive, cable and protection records.
5. Compare findings with approved nameplate/configuration/engineering limits.
6. Repair verified cause using approved parts and work instruction.
7. Restore guards and complete authorized return-to-service checks.
8. Conduct controlled test and review current/load trend.

**Close-out:** document verified cause, work order, protective-device status, test result and authorized acceptance. Escalate smoke, exposed conductors, burning smell, repeat trips or uncertain energy state.
