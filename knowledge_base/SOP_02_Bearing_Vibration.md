# Simulated SOP — Bearing Vibration
**ID:** SIM-SOP-BRG-001 | **Rev:** 0.1 | **Training / PoC only**

> Training example only. Actual vibration limits and measurement criteria must come from approved OEM/site standards.

**Trigger:** PLC_M241, elevated vibration signal or unusual noise.

**Operator response**
1. Record asset, alarm, timestamp, operating condition and recent speed/load changes.
2. Observe from safe distance; do not touch rotating equipment.
3. Note noise, heat indication, leakage or visible movement.
4. Notify maintenance; escalate severe or rapidly worsening vibration.
5. Do not lubricate, adjust or remove guards unless specifically authorized.

**Authorized maintenance**
1. Follow approved shutdown, isolation and LOTO before intrusive work.
2. Review trends under comparable speed/load conditions.
3. Inspect housing, mounting, lubrication, coupling and alignment per OEM.
4. Verify sensor mounting and instrument health.
5. Use approved measurement method and OEM acceptance criteria.
6. Repair under approved work instruction.
7. Reassemble, guard and obtain return-to-service authorization.
8. Capture post-maintenance readings and verify trend.

**Close-out:** record cause, measurement method, work performed and post-work result.
